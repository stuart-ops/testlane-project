Energy

A valuation model that keeps itself up to date

A listed North Sea energy producer replaced a hand-maintained spreadsheet with a live internal dashboard.

The problem

The company valued its assets in a spreadsheet. Whenever commodity prices moved, the accounting data changed or a process was adjusted, someone had to update the model by hand. Rerunning it for a board meeting took hours, and the numbers were out of date as soon as it was finished.

The data is commercially sensitive, so putting it into an off-the-shelf online analytics tool was not an option.

What we did

We built a private, live dashboard that runs the full valuation: production, hedging, tax, cash and dividends. Prices refresh automatically each night. When processes or the accounting system change, the model follows them, with no rebuild and no retyping.

If a data source sends something that looks wrong, the dashboard ignores it and carries on with the last good figures, and flags anything that has gone stale. In the meeting, directors can move prices, drilling plans and payout and see the answer straight away.

Which AI did what

  • The valuation engine itself is plain code, so the numbers are predictable and repeatable. We do not let an AI guess a valuation.
  • A reasoning model helped design the model and then reviewed it, looking for logic errors before it went live.
