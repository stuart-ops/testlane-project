Property

Never missing a certificate renewal

A tracking system for a property portfolio's legal paperwork.

The problem

Letting property means a steady stream of deadlines: gas safety certificates, electrical reports, energy ratings, insurance renewals and deposit protection. Missing one can mean fines of thousands of pounds. Tracking them in spreadsheets and email folders is how things slip.

What we did

Documents that arrive by email are read automatically, and the key details are picked out: what it is, which property, and when it expires. The file is then stored in the right place and added to a deadline tracker.

Warnings go out at 30, 14 and 3 days before anything expires, and they keep coming until someone confirms it is dealt with. Everything is kept on the owner's own storage, not in an online document service.

Which AI did what

  • Text recognition reads scanned certificates and reports.
  • A reasoning model works out what each document is, which property it belongs to and when it expires.
