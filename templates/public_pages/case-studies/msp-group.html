Finance

Group accounts without the monthly scramble

A managed services group with four entities in three countries now consolidates itself every night.

The problem

Four entities kept their books in separate accounting systems, in five currencies. Each month, consolidating them took five to seven days of spreadsheet work, and the management pack was hard to check.

What we did

We built a secure reporting system that collects the figures from every entity overnight, matches up the transactions between group companies, and produces a consolidated view. Every adjustment is recorded, and every figure can be reviewed and queried by the right person.

Leadership looks at the same live screen, with each person seeing only what they should.
