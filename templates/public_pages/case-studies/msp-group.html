Case Study 01

Transatlantic MSP Group — Multi-Entity QBO → BigQuery Warehouse

Global Managed Service Provider — $16M ARR — Transatlantic Group

Client Profile

Global Managed Service Provider with entities across three jurisdictions: US Parent LLC, UK Limited Company, UAE Free Zone Company. Multi-currency operations spanning five currencies with monthly group consolidation requirements.

Problem

Four entities posting to separate QuickBooks Online instances. Monthly group consolidation required 5–7 days of manual spreadsheet work. Management pack preparation was labour-intensive and error-prone. No unified view of group financial position.

Sovereign Solution

LayerImplementation
IngestionNightly Cloud Run job pulls GL, invoices, expenses from all four QBO realms via OAuth 2.0. Credentials in Google Secret Manager.
WarehouseBigQuery datasets: raw extract and reconciled reporting marts (PL summary, consolidated PL, customer margin, trial balance).
Normalisation603 adjustment lines across entities. Five accounting bases tracked: QBO posted, normalised, proforma, pack normalised, workbook posted.
ConsolidationLive intercompany elimination engine. Residuals flagged to intercompany unreconciled account.
DashboardFlask app behind Cloudflare Access OTP with role-based views and permission boundaries.
PerimeterCloud Run internal ingress, Cloud NAT fixed egress IP. Zero public internet exposure.
Review LoopThumbs up/down on every P&L figure with reviewer identity tracking. Nightly sync re-checks open items.
