Case Study 04

Sovereign Property & Compliance Vault

Statutory Compliance Tracking & Audit-Ready Document Management

Problem

Property compliance involves dozens of deadline-driven documents across multiple properties: gas safety certificates, EICR electrical reports, EPC ratings, insurance renewals, tenancy deposit protections. Missing a single renewal triggers statutory penalties (£3,000+ for gas safety, £5,000+ for deposit protection breaches). Manual tracking across spreadsheets and email folders is fragile and error-prone.

Sovereign Solution

LayerImplementation
Email IngestionHermes Agent scans Gmail inbox for compliance documents: certificates, reports, council correspondence, tenancy agreements.
OCR PipelinePDF attachments processed through local OCR (Tesseract) extracting: document type, property address, issue date, expiry date, issuing body.
Deadline RadarDuckDB local database tracking compliance events with approaching deadline detection. Daily cron check.
Auto-FilingIncoming documents auto-sorted into structured filesystem. Syncthing mirrors to phone for on-site access.
AlertingTelegram notification at 30, 14, and 3 days before each deadline. Escalation if unacknowledged.
StorageAll documents on local NAS filesystem. Zero cloud document storage.

Outcome

Complete compliance oversight with zero missed deadlines. Documents auto-filed and accessible on-site via phone. Telegram alerts provide peace of mind without requiring constant manual calendar checking. All sensitive property and tenant documents remain on local hardware — never in a cloud document store.
