Governance

Board minutes without the audio leaving the building

Meetings transcribed and minuted on the client's own equipment.

The problem

Boardrooms and legal discussions produce recordings that cannot safely be sent to an online transcription service. Using one risks confidentiality and, in some cases, legal privilege. The alternative is a person typing minutes from memory and scribbled notes days later.

What we did

Meetings are recorded on a phone and the file moves straight to a server inside the client's own office. The recording is turned into text there, drafted into minutes with a clear list of actions, and sent to the right people as a PDF.

At no point does the audio pass through a third party. Actions go straight onto the client's own task board, so nothing gets lost between the meeting and the follow-up.

Which AI did what

  • A speech model on the client's own equipment turns the recording into text.
  • A reasoning model drafts the minutes and the actions list from that text.
