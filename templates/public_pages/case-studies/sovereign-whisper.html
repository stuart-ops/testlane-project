Case Study 03

Sovereign Whisper — On-Premise Boardroom Intelligence

100% On-Premise Meeting Transcription & Minutes

Problem

Enterprise boardrooms and legal proceedings generate sensitive audio that cannot be sent to cloud transcription services. Regulatory and litigation privilege requires complete audio isolation. Existing solutions (cloud-based transcription, human transcription services) all involve audio leaving the building.

Sovereign Solution

LayerImplementation
CaptureVoice memos recorded on company iPhones. Syncthing synchronises audio directly to NAS — no iCloud, Google Drive, or Dropbox intermediary.
TranscriptionLocal Whisper model (faster-whisper) running on NAS Docker container. GPU-accelerated if available. Zero audio bytes leave the network.
MinutesHermes Agent processes transcript into structured executive minutes using local LLM inference or dedicated API key.
Action ItemsStructured JSON output pushed to Obsidian Kanban via local filesystem. No Asana/Jira/Notion dependency.
DeliveryMinutes PDF generated locally via ReportLab, emailed via client's own Gmail API.
PerimeterTailscale WireGuard mesh. NAS behind corporate firewall. No open inbound ports.

Key Differentiator

Unlike cloud transcription services where audio transits third-party infrastructure, Sovereign Whisper keeps all audio processing on the client's own hardware. This is the only approach that satisfies legal privilege requirements for boardroom and litigation recordings.
